# Profile Headline Formulas

**Character limit:** 220 (confirm current LinkedIn specs before applying — this changes with redesigns). Use all of them.

**Truncation is shorter than 220 in practice.** Search results, comment bylines and the mobile card all cut the headline off well before the full field, so the opening few words are doing more work than the rest. Put `[What You Do]` first and don't rely on anything past the first `|` to land in those surfaces.

## The formula

```
[What You Do] | [Who You Help] [Achieve What Result]
```

Three parts separated by `|`. Each part does one job:
- **What You Do** — your actual function, not your title
- **Who You Help** — the specific audience
- **Achieve What Result** — the concrete outcome

## Rules

1. **Lead with value, not title.** "Helping B2B founders book 40% more demos" beats "VP of Marketing".
2. **Be specific about who you help.** "B2B SaaS founders 1-10M ARR" beats "business owners".
3. **Include keywords recruiters search for.** LinkedIn indexes the headline heavily, so a search-matched headline surfaces in meaningfully more recruiter results than one built around a title alone — no traceable source for an exact multiplier, so don't quote one to a client.
4. **No filler adjectives.** Cut "passionate", "driven", "results-oriented".
5. **Capitalize names, products, companies** — "Claude", "HubSpot", "Salesforce".

## Before → After

### For a marketer

- ❌ "Marketing Manager at Company X"
- ✅ "Helping B2B SaaS companies close 40% more deals | Sales Director at TechCorp"

### For a founder

- ❌ "Founder & CEO"
- ✅ "Building tools that help creators monetize | Founder at CreatorLab"

### For a consultant

- ❌ "Marketing Consultant"
- ✅ "I help service businesses get clients without ads | Marketing Consultant"

### For a developer

- ❌ "Senior Software Engineer"
- ✅ "Shipping AI agents for B2B ops teams | Senior Engineer at StackCo"

### For an executive

- ❌ "CTO | VP Engineering | Technology Leader"
- ✅ "Helping fintechs scale past $100M ARR | CTO at PaymentCo"

### For a senior employee, not a founder (no P&L of their own)

- ❌ "Operations Manager at Grupo Industrial X"
- ✅ "Redesenhando a operação de estoque para fábricas de médio porte | Gerente de Operações na Grupo Industrial X"

### For someone outside tech (retail/industry)

- ❌ "Gerente Comercial na Rede de Varejo Y"
- ✅ "Ajudo redes de varejo a recuperar margem sem cortar equipe | Gerente Comercial na Rede Y"

## Anti-patterns (auto-fail)

- "Passionate thought leader" — empty signal
- "Driven professional" — empty
- "10+ years of experience in..." — nobody searches this
- All-caps headlines
- Emoji chains (🚀🔥💡) — read as low-effort
- Generic "Open to Work" framing (move that to the banner badge, not headline)

## Search-keyword placement

LinkedIn search indexes the headline heavily. If you want to appear in search for a role or topic:
- Include the target role noun ("CTO", "Head of Marketing", "AI Consultant")
- Include the target industry ("B2B SaaS", "Healthcare IT", "FinTech")
- Include your specialty keyword ("RAG", "agentic workflows", "demand gen")

Profiles with 5+ target keywords in the headline + About get meaningfully more recruiter search impressions — directional, no traceable source for a specific multiplier.
